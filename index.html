ℹ️ About FLOODCAST

FLOODCAST is a research prototype that uses real-time weather observations and mathematical concepts to examine changing weather conditions associated with possible localized flooding.

Its risk indicator is experimental. It is not a verified flood prediction or an official warning system. Always follow PAGASA and local disaster-management advisories.
